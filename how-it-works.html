The Journey

Your 6-step transformation.

A certified phlebotomist collects your blood samples at your doorstep — across Gachibowli, Jubilee Hills, Madhapur, Hitec City, Secunderabad, Kondapur and nearby areas.

02

Fast Digital Lab Reports

Your diagnostic results are delivered to WhatsApp and Email within 24 hours — clear, readable, and ready to discuss.

03

10-Minute Doctor Consultation

A physician reviews your report over telehealth video or phone and answers all your specific medical queries.

04

Custom Care Plan

Receive a personalized diet plan, targeted supplement recommendations, and prescription medications if needed.

05

Weekly Accountability

For 4–12 weeks, our team checks in regularly to keep your diet adherence and supplement routine on track.

06

3-Month Retest & 1-Year Analytics

A 90-day progress retest leads into a 1-year visual health trend graph so you can see exactly how far you’ve come.

BEFORE YOUR SAMPLE

Pre-test fasting guidelines

  • ✓ Fast for 8–10 hours overnight before your collection.
  • ✓ Drink plenty of water — hydration helps the draw.
  • ✓ Avoid alcohol and vigorous exercise for 24 hours prior.
  • ✓ Take your regular morning medicines unless advised otherwise.

AFTER YOUR CARE JOURNEY

Retest, track, and see the trend.

At the 3-month mark you retake your core lab panel to measure real progress. Results feed into a 1-year visual health trend graph — your personal proof that small, consistent changes compound.
